# Procedencia de los recursos

## Visualizaciones conceptuales originales

Generadas con la herramienta de imagen integrada. No corresponden a una obra real ni a una realización de ROK Atelier.

- `hero-concept.webp`, `hero-concept-mobile.webp`: residencia conceptual de concreto y piedra, jardín y espejo de agua, luz de tarde. Prompt: «A monolithic dark warm grey board-formed concrete and natural ochre limestone house in a subtle highland landscape, interlocking volumes framing a recessed living space, olive tree, still reflecting pool, raking amber afternoon light, architectural editorial photography, no people, text or logos.»
- `atelier-concept.webp`: interior conceptual, derivado de la misma dirección visual. Prompt: «Interior of the same imaginary minimalist Mexican residence, floating oak staircase against a tall raw concrete wall, dramatic diagonal beam from a rectangular skylight, limestone floor, courtyard with olive tree, dark timber dining table, tactile materials, architectural editorial photography, no people, text or logos.»
